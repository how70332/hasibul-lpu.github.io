Biography

Md Hasibul Alam is currently pursuing his MA in Counselling Psychology at the School of Psychology, Lovely Professional University (LPU), India (2024–Present). A dedicated researcher bridging clinical neuroscience and positive psychology, his work explores the intersection of brain health, emotional well-being, and therapeutic intervention.

His primary research focus is on Alzheimer's disease biomarkers, contributing to the international study on plasma and neuroimaging markers (PMID: 41499177). He is also investigating gratitude-based interventions for psychosis, and the science of mindfulness and happiness, with research interests closely aligned with the American Psychological Association's 2025 themes on well-being and preventive mental health.

Hasibul holds a BSc in Psychology from International Open University (2024) and an MBA in Marketing from Stamford University Bangladesh (2006), bringing an interdisciplinary perspective to mental health research. He completed a 15-day psychology observation internship at Fortis Hospital, gaining practical exposure to hospital-based psychological care. He is committed to advancing evidence-based counselling practices in South Asia.
